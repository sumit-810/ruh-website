# Research Evidence Log

> **Note (31 Oct 2026):** this log comes from the first research phase, carried out while the brand used a retired working name. The brand is now **Gentle Company**. The market, pricing and legal evidence still applies.
> - The current competitor study and design direction are in [`RESEARCH_AND_DESIGN_STRATEGY.md`](RESEARCH_AND_DESIGN_STRATEGY.md).
> - Section D compares the *first* implementation, which has since been redesigned.

Companion to `RESEARCH_AND_BRAND_STRATEGY.md`. This log records **what was actually accessed**, how, and what was observed. Findings feed directly into the design decisions noted in the last column.

Research date: **30 October 2026** (prices and promotions were observed during the Indian Diwali sale period, so many "sale" prices are below MRP).

## Method and tools — what was and was not possible

| Tool requested | Status in this session | What was done instead |
|---|---|---|
| Firecrawl | **Available, used** | Remote rendering of 18 homepages and 6 product pages: full-page desktop screenshots (1440 px), mobile screenshots (360 px), structured extraction (JSON), and web/news search for market data. |
| Claude in Chrome | **Not connected** in this session | Visual inspection was done on the Firecrawl screenshots (downloaded and viewed as images: hero crops plus full-page thumbnail strips). |
| Direct browser / Playwright on competitor sites | **Blocked**: the session's network policy returned HTTP 403 for aesop.com, rhodeskin.com, soldejaneiro.com, byredo.com, forestessentialsindia.com, kamaayurveda.com, beminimalist.co and unsplash.com | Playwright was used for our own site's QA only (localhost). |
| "Frontend Design" tool | **Not available** in this session | The design direction was developed manually from the evidence below (see `BRAND_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`). |
| Context7 | Available | Not needed: the site uses only web-platform APIs (no framework). |

**Reliability note.** Firecrawl's JSON extraction is produced by a language model. Some extracted fields were visibly wrong. For example, the Byredo extraction listed "Customer Reviews" and "Vegan certification" that were not on the page. Only observations confirmed in the screenshots, or text quoted directly from page content, are used below. Unverifiable extraction output was discarded.

### Pages that could not be studied properly (honest limitations)

- **Kama Ayurveda** (kamaayurveda.com): the render returned only a store-selection / sign-in modal over a botanical image. The homepage layout could not be assessed.
- **Plum** product page (`/products/vanilla-vibes-body-mist-by-plum-bodylovin`): redirected to the homepage for the remote renderer. Plum's price came from search results and Nykaa instead.
- **Rhode** homepage: the full-page capture returned only one mid-page viewport. The mobile capture link expired before download.
- **Laneige** (laneige.com) served the Korean site, so it was used only for visual reference.
- **Mamaearth** (mamaearth.in) redirected to the international mamaearth.com store.
- **Fenty Skin** (fentyskin.com) redirected to fentybeauty.com/pages/fenty-skin.
- **Aesop** homepage: imagery was lazy-loaded and appeared as placeholders in the full-page capture. Layout, typography and copy were still assessable, and the mobile capture showed the real hero.
- **Reddit** threads cannot be scraped by Firecrawl. Consumer sentiment from Reddit relies on **search-result excerpts only** and is treated as anecdotal.

Our build uses concept renders, no human imagery, no reviews and no loyalty scheme, because none of those can be created without real assets or data. The structure for each is ready (gallery, reviews section, config slots).
